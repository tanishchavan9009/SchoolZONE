# SAFEZONE 360 — The Future of School Road Safety
## Human-Centered Product Innovation (MDM 15) &bull; PCCOE Pune

**Tagline:** *One Safer Street. A Thousand Brighter Tomorrows.*

### Overview
This project is an interactive, production-ready civic technology digital experience built around the research presentation *“School-Zone Road Safety”* conducted for Formative Assessment FA-1 at **Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune** under the guidance of **Dr. Vrushali Bhalerao**.

### Core Sections & Features
1. **Cinematic Hero Stage**: Full-screen 3D perspective intersection showing PCCOE campus entrance, autonomous moving vehicles, respectful walking students, scanning radar beams, and floating telemetry indicators.
2. **Interactive Top-Down Simulator**: Playable digital twin demonstration featuring:
   - Speed enforcement toggle (20 km/h radar restriction)
   - Pedestrian crossing signal toggle (synchronized right-of-way)
   - Drop-off lane organization toggle (dedicated single-file bay vs. hazardous curbside idling)
   - School-zone warning sign toggle
   - Preset buttons: *Chaotic Peak-Hour*, *SafeZone 360 System*, and *Reset Defaults*
   - Dynamic real-time telemetry readouts: Traffic Congestion, Vehicle Flow, Pedestrian Crossing, and Pickup Zone
3. **Research Dashboard**:
   - Primary empirical stat: **44 responses/comments analyzed** in primary Excel sheet
   - Dual qualitative streams: College student perspectives (PCCOE) and public Reddit discussions
   - 5-step data analysis methodology: *Review &rarr; Coding &rarr; Categorization &rarr; Pattern Identification &rarr; Insight Generation*
   - Expandable details for all 6 core categories from the PDF: Traffic & Speeding, Parking Issues, Pedestrian Safety, Safety Awareness, Stakeholder Coordination, and Infrastructure Gaps
   - Qualitative priority matrix (without fabricated percentages)
4. **Empathy Lab**: Interactive hexagon-style interconnected panels for **SAYS, THINKS, DOES, FEELS, PAINS, GAINS** with interactive modal inspect views.
5. **Persona Explorer**: Deep profiles for:
   - **Dr. Rahul Chaudhari** (Faculty / Civil Engineering)
   - **Kranti Khade** (SY B.Tech / Computer Engineering)
   - **Anushka Kale** (SY B.Tech / Civil Engineering)
6. **Customer Journey Map**: 5-stage interactive stepper: *Leaving Home &rarr; Traveling Roads &rarr; Crossing Roads &rarr; School Zone &rarr; Drop-off / Arrival*.
7. **Before & After Experience**: Interactive drag-and-touch comparison slider between Disorganized School Zone and Designed for Safety.
8. **Solutions & Future Vision**: 6 solution cards including the conceptual *Safe School Zone Companion App*.
9. **Research Limitations & Transparency**: Academic acknowledgment of the 44-comment qualitative sample size, subjective bias, and digital representation limits.
10. **Audio Synthesizer**: Native Web Audio API audio feedback toggle with zero external dependencies.
